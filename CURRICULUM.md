# CURRICULUM — A2 → B1 in five arcs

Dialect baseline: urban Palestinian koine (Jerusalem/Ramallah). Gazan variants taught explicitly in Arc 3. Register tags used throughout: `street` (dialect), `news` (journalistic), `shared` (MSA-borrowed nouns used inside dialect speech — the political lexicon mostly lives here).

Pacing assumption: 15–20 min/day. A lesson spans 1–2 days (intro day + consolidation day); a unit ≈ 2 weeks. New vocabulary ≈ 8–10 items/lesson, ~55/unit, **~1,100 items across the trajectory** on top of the known several hundred → B1-range lexicon (~1,500–2,000).

**Lesson anatomy (every lesson, 15–20 min):**
1. **مراجعة** Review queue — 3 min of SRS (sentences + audio first).
2. **كلمات** New vocab — 8–10 items, audio, Hebrew gloss + cognate flags.
3. **Core block** — dialogue *or* listening clip, scaffolded (pre-questions → chunked listening → transcript reveal → gloss).
4. **Check** — cloze, MCQ on meaning *heard not read*, reorder, or dictation (Arc 3+).
5. **جسر** Cognate corner — 1–2 Hebrew bridges with the true/shifted/false-friend story.
6. Wrap — new items auto-enqueue into SRS; "can-do" line shown.

**Unit anatomy:** 5 content lessons + **L6: مراجعة واستماع حر** — cumulative review + one real, unsimplified clip (the unit's proof of progress).

**Harakat policy:** Arc 1 fully vowelized · Arc 2 first-appearance only · Arcs 3–5 unvowelized except disambiguation.

## Arc 1 — الشارع · The Street (Units 1–4)
*Mission: turn passive A2 knowledge into real-time comprehension. Familiar topics at native speed — the speed is the curriculum.*

**Grammar thread:** b-imperfect (بحكي), عم progressive, رح future, ما…ش negation, إضافة & تبع possession, object suffixes (قلّي، حكيتله), أيش/شو question battery.

| Unit | Title | Focus | Free-listening target (L6) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **شو صار؟** · What happened? | The "news of the day" frame in everyday talk: صار، سمع، شاف، حكى; question words; reacting (والله؟ عنجد؟ يا حرام). Sets up the entire news arc. | A 60–90s vox-pop clip, street Arabic |
| 2 | **أهل وشغل** · Family & work at speed | Family, professions, daily routine — known ground, pushed to listening fluency; numbers/ages/times by ear. | Self-introduction monologue, native speed |
| 3 | **عالطريق** · On the road | Taxi/serveece, directions, الحاجز (the checkpoint as daily reality — procedural language: هويّة، تفتيش، طابور، استنّى), city names as pronounced (القدس، رام الله، الخليل، نابلس، غزة). | Dashcam-style checkpoint/taxi conversation |
| 4 | **السوق والقهوة** · Market & coffee | Buying, haggling, quantities, food staples; hospitality scripts (تفضّل، صحتين); polite refusal. | Market vendor interview |

**Arc exit can-do:** follow a 90-second everyday conversation between two Palestinians at natural speed without transcript.

## Arc 2 — الأخبار · The News (Units 5–9)
*Mission: enter the news register. Learn how Palestinians talk ABOUT events — and how broadcast Arabic frames them. Diglossia taught explicitly from Unit 5.*

**Grammar thread:** كان + عم past narration; sequencing (لما، بعدين، قبل ما); relative إللي; verbal nouns (مصدر) as news currency (اعتقال، إطلاق، اقتحام); passive-voice forms for events (انقتل، انعتقل، تهدّم); reported speech (قال إنّه).

| Unit | Title | Focus | Free-listening target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | **خبر عاجل** · Breaking news | News meta-language: خبر، مصدر، عاجل، حسب; the anchor-vs-street register split, explicitly; following a headline read in MSA then discussed in dialect. | Headline block + street reactions |
| 6 | **الضفة** · The West Bank | Occupation daily lexicon: احتلال، مستوطنة/مستوطن، اقتحام، اعتقال، حاجز، تصريح; geography of Areas A/B/C as people actually reference them. | News package on a settlement expansion |
| 7 | **القدس** · Jerusalem | The city's vocabulary: البلدة القديمة، الأقصى، الشيخ جرّاح; house demolitions (هدم); residency/ID regime; Jerusalemite dialect notes. | Interview with a Jerusalemite resident |
| 8 | **مظاهرة** · Demonstration | Protest language: مظاهرة، مسيرة، هتاف، شعار، قمع، غاز مسيل للدموع; understanding chanted/rhythmic speech; crowd audio strategies. | Protest footage + participant interviews |
| 9 | **أرقام وتواريخ** · Numbers & dates | The numeric skeleton of news: casualties, percentages, dates, years (٤٨، ٦٧ as cultural shorthand); fast number comprehension drills by ear. | News summary dense with figures |

**Arc exit can-do:** watch a 2-minute Palestinian news package and state who did what to whom, where, when.

## Arc 3 — غزة · Gaza (Units 10–13)
*Mission: the register of the war — testimony, dispatches, humanitarian language. The professionally essential arc. Lessons are labeled for emotional weight; everyday-life material stays interleaved (Gaza is also a place where people cook, joke, and queue for bread — the units reflect that, which is itself the non-banal point).*

**Grammar thread:** dictation introduced; unvowelized text becomes default; intensifiers and emotional discourse markers (والله العظيم، يا ريت، الحمد لله rituals under duress); Gazan dialect notes (ق realizations, vocabulary items that differ from West Bank koine).

| Unit | Title | Focus | Free-listening target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | **الحرب** · The war | Core war lexicon: حرب، قصف، غارة، صاروخ، دمار، شهيد، جريح، نزوح; how testimony differs from reportage grammatically (first person, present-tense reliving). | Survivor testimony excerpt (labeled heavy) |
| 11 | **النزوح** · Displacement | خيمة، إخلاء، ملجأ، عيلة مشتّتة; movement orders; the vocabulary of waiting; Rafah/Khan Younis/Jabalia as pronounced by Gazans. | Displaced family interview |
| 12 | **الجوع والمساعدات** · Hunger & aid | مجاعة، طحين، مساعدات، تكية، معبر، شاحنة; aid-distribution discourse; prices as spoken (a kilo of flour costs…). | Dispatch from an aid queue |
| 13 | **وقف إطلاق النار** · Ceasefire | Negotiation discourse: وقف إطلاق النار، هدنة، صفقة، تبادل أسرى، انسحاب; hope/skepticism markers; journalists' sign-offs and dispatch structure. | Gazan journalist piece-to-camera |

**Arc exit can-do:** follow a 3-minute unsubtitled testimony or dispatch from Gaza and summarize it accurately in English.

## Arc 4 — السياسة · Politics (Units 14–17)
*Mission: the discourse layer — actors, institutions, argument. Understanding not just what happened but how Palestinians argue about it.*

**Grammar thread:** conditionals (إذا، لو + counterfactuals — the grammar of political argument); concession (مع إنّه، رغم); opinion frames (برأيي، بصراحة، المشكلة إنّه); hedging vs emphasis.

| Unit | Title | Focus | Free-listening target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14 | **مين بيحكم؟** · Who rules? | السلطة، حكومة، رئيس، انتخابات، فساد; factions (فتح، حماس، الجبهة) as referenced in speech; the انقسام (split). | Street debate on the PA |
| 15 | **الأسرى** · Prisoners | أسير/أسرى، معتقل، إضراب عن الطعام، إداري (administrative detention), تحرير، صفقة; prisoner families' discourse — a register of its own. | Released prisoner / family interview |
| 16 | **النكبة والذاكرة** · Nakba & memory | نكبة، لاجئ، مخيّم، عودة، مفتاح; how memory vocabulary surfaces in *current* politics (right of return in today's speech). *(Politics-of-memory angle — distinct from the deferred oral-history arc.)* | Refugee-camp resident on return |
| 17 | **العالم بيتفرّج** · The world watches | International layer as spoken: أمريكا، الأمم المتحدة، قرار، فيتو، تطبيع، مقاطعة; sarcasm and bitter irony — the hardest register to hear correctly. | Commentary/satire clip |

**Arc exit can-do:** follow a two-speaker political disagreement and articulate both positions.

## Arc 5 — أصوات · Voices (Units 18–20)
*Mission: remove the scaffolding. Long-form, unscripted, multi-speaker. The app stops being a teacher and becomes a gym.*

| Unit | Title | Focus | Free-listening target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 18 | **بودكاست** · Podcast | 10–15 min podcast episodes in Levantine; note-taking while listening; tolerance for unknown words (gist strategies, repair: المهم إنّه…). | Full podcast episode |
| 19 | **مقابلة طويلة** · The long interview | 20+ min interviews; speaker-switching, interruptions, dialect mixing (guests from Gaza/WB/48/diaspora in one conversation). | Full TV/YouTube interview |
| 20 | **الامتحان** · Exit | Three unscaffolded pieces (one street, one news, one testimony) + self-assessment against B1 can-dos; graduation report; v2 trajectory proposed based on error profile. | All three, cold |

**Trajectory exit criteria (B1 listening):** understands the main points and important details of connected speech on familiar-by-now topics (daily life, news, the war, politics) delivered at natural speed in Palestinian Arabic, including identifying speaker stance; can follow a 10-minute interview with ≥80% comprehension of propositions.

## Cross-cutting systems

- **Onboarding triage:** before Unit 1, a known/unknown swipe through the ~600-word A2 Levantine core. Known → long SRS intervals; unknown → injected into Arc 1 lessons' review slots.
- **Cognate ledger:** every taught word carries a Hebrew-bridge flag; the ledger page collects them (true bridges, shifted-meaning stories, false friends) — a reference that compounds.
- **Register meter:** each lesson shows its street/news/shared mix; over the trajectory the meter visibly shifts — the trajectory made visible.
- **Listening Lab (v1):** all free-listening clips plus extras, filterable by arc/length/register, replayable with scaffold layers — turns finished units into a permanent comprehension library.
